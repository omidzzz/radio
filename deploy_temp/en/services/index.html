Treatment Journey

Your Treatment Journey in Four Steps

01 / 04

From your first consultation to post-treatment follow-ups, we are with you at every stage.

  1. 1
    STEP 01

    Consultation & Assessment

    A radiation oncologist reviews your medical history and imaging to determine the most suitable treatment option.

  2. 2
    STEP 02

    Imaging & Planning

    CT simulation and advanced imaging create a precise, fully personalized treatment plan (contouring and plan design).

  3. 3
    STEP 03

    Treatment Sessions

    Short 15–30 minute sessions on state-of-the-art equipment with continuous monitoring by our team.

  4. 4
    STEP 04

    Follow-up & Care

    Control imaging, treatment response review, and ongoing support after your course is complete.
